# UNIVERSITY ACADEMIC REGULATIONS AND STUDENT POLICY HANDBOOK
Document Identifier: REG-2026-V4
Target Audience: All Undergraduate and Postgraduate Students, Faculty, and Academic Counselors
Effective Academic Year: 2026-2027

================================================================================
SECTION 1: ACADEMIC INTEGRITY AND HONESTY CODE
================================================================================
Clause 1.1 - Definition of Plagiarism and Unauthorized Collaboration:
Academic integrity is foundational to the university's mission. Plagiarism is defined as the representation of another person's words, thoughts, software code, mathematical proofs, or artistic creations as one's own without appropriate citation. Unauthorized collaboration refers to working with another student or third party on any graded assignment, quiz, or examination without explicit permission from the instructor of record.

Clause 1.2 - Penalties for First-Time Violations:
When an instructor suspects a first-time violation, the evidence must be submitted to the Departmental Ethics Board within ten (10) business days. For minor assignments (weighted under 15% of course grade), the student shall receive a grade of zero (0) on the assignment. For major assignments or mid-term examinations, the penalty is a mandatory failing grade (F) for the entire course. All first offenses are recorded confidentially in the Dean of Students registry.

Clause 1.3 - Subsequent and Repeat Violations:
A second verified violation of academic integrity shall result in automatic referral to the University Disciplinary Committee. Penalties for repeat offenses include mandatory suspension for one to two academic semesters, or permanent expulsion from the university. Expulsions are permanently noted on official academic transcripts.

Clause 1.4 - Ethics Appeal Procedures:
Students may appeal any academic integrity sanction within seven (7) business days of receiving written notification. The written appeal must cite either procedural irregularities in the board's investigation or newly discovered material evidence. Appeals are adjudicated by the Vice Provost for Academic Affairs, whose decision is final.

SECTION 2: COURSE REGISTRATION, ADD/DROP, AND WITHDRAWAL POLICIES
================================================================================
Clause 2.1 - Regular Add/Drop Period:
Students may add or drop semester-length courses during the first twelve (12) calendar days of each semester without financial penalty or transcript notation. Courses dropped during this window are completely removed from the permanent student record.

Clause 2.2 - Course Withdrawal (Grade of 'W'):
From calendar day thirteen (13) through the end of the eighth (8th) academic week of the semester, students may formally withdraw from a course via the Student Information Portal. A grade of 'W' (Withdrawn) will be recorded on the official transcript. The grade of 'W' carries zero grade point weight and does not impact cumulative GPA. Students are limited to a lifetime maximum of four (4) 'W' grades across their undergraduate degree program.

Clause 2.3 - Emergency and Medical Withdrawal:
Students facing severe medical emergencies, hospitalization, or documented family tragedies may petition the Dean of Academic Advising for an Emergency Medical Withdrawal. Petitions must be submitted within thirty (30) days of the triggering event with certified physician documentation. Approved medical withdrawals grant 'WM' grades across all courses for the term without counting toward the 4-withdrawal lifetime cap.

Clause 2.4 - Course Overload Requirements:
The standard full-time course load is twelve (12) to eighteen (18) credit hours per semester. Students wishing to register for nineteen (19) to twenty-one (21) credit hours must possess a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.50 and receive written approval from their academic advisor. Registrations exceeding twenty-one (21) credit hours are strictly prohibited.

SECTION 3: GRADING SYSTEM, GRADE POINTS, AND GPA CALCULATION
================================================================================
Clause 3.1 - Standard Grading Scale:
The university utilizes a four-point (4.00) grading scale:
Grade A+ : 4.00 Grade Points (97 - 100%)
Grade A  : 4.00 Grade Points (93 - 96%)
Grade A- : 3.70 Grade Points (90 - 92%)
Grade B+ : 3.30 Grade Points (87 - 89%)
Grade B  : 3.00 Grade Points (83 - 86%)
Grade B- : 2.70 Grade Points (80 - 82%)
Grade C+ : 2.30 Grade Points (77 - 79%)
Grade C  : 2.00 Grade Points (73 - 76%)
Grade C- : 1.70 Grade Points (70 - 72%)
Grade D  : 1.00 Grade Points (60 - 69%)
Grade F  : 0.00 Grade Points (Below 60%, Failure)

Clause 3.2 - Incomplete Grades ('I'):
An Incomplete grade ('I') may be granted only when a student has completed at least 70% of course coursework with a passing grade but cannot complete the final examination or final project due to documented extenuating circumstances. The remaining coursework must be submitted no later than six (6) weeks after the start of the following semester. If uncompleted, the 'I' grade automatically converts to an 'F'.

Clause 3.3 - Course Repetition Policy:
A student may repeat a course in which they earned a grade of C- or lower. A course may be repeated at most twice (three attempts total). Only the highest grade earned is calculated in the cumulative GPA, although all attempts remain visible on the official transcript.

Clause 3.4 - Dean's Honor List Qualifications:
At the conclusion of each fall and spring semester, the Dean's Honor List is published. To qualify, an undergraduate student must complete a minimum of twelve (12) graded credit hours with a semester GPA of 3.75 or higher, and have no incomplete ('I') or failing ('F') grades on record for that semester.

SECTION 4: ATTENDANCE, PARTICIPATION, AND LEAVE OF ABSENCE
================================================================================
Clause 4.1 - Mandatory Attendance Thresholds:
Regular attendance is compulsory for all laboratory sessions, clinical practicums, and lecture sections. Students who accumulate unexcused absences exceeding fifteen percent (15%) of total scheduled contact hours may be involuntarily withdrawn from the course with an administrative grade of 'FA' (Failure due to Absences).

Clause 4.2 - Excused Absences:
Absences are excused for verified medical illness with clinic note, participation in official university athletic competitions, mandatory military service, or observance of recognized religious holidays. Notification must be provided to the course instructor in writing at least forty-eight (48) hours prior to anticipated religious or athletic absences.

Clause 4.3 - Temporary Leave of Absence:
A student in good academic standing (GPA 2.00 or higher) may request a formal Leave of Absence (LOA) for up to two (2) consecutive semesters. LOA requests must be filed with the Registrar's Office before the end of the second week of classes. Students on approved LOA retain their university email and library access but are ineligible for campus housing or student employment.

================================================================================
SECTION 5: GRADUATION AND DEGREE CONFERRAL REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================
Clause 5.1 - Credit Hour Requirements:
To qualify for a Bachelor of Science (B.S.) or Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) degree, students must successfully complete a minimum of one hundred twenty (120) semester credit hours, including all General Education core curricula and major specialization requirements.

Clause 5.2 - Minimum GPA for Graduation:
Students must attain a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.00 in all university coursework, as well as a minimum major GPA of 2.25 in all courses applied toward their primary major.

Clause 5.3 - Residency Requirement:
At least thirty (30) of the final thirty-six (36) semester credit hours must be completed in direct residence at the home campus. Credits obtained through examination or credit-by-portfolio do not satisfy the institutional residency requirement.

Clause 5.4 - Application for Graduation:
Candidates for graduation must submit a formal Graduation Application via the student portal at least five (5) months prior to the scheduled commencement ceremony. Failure to submit on time will postpone formal degree conferral to the subsequent academic cycle.

================================================================================
SECTION 6: TUITION FEES, PAYMENT DEADLINES, AND REFUND SCHEDULES
================================================================================
Clause 6.1 - Payment Deadlines:
Tuition and mandatory student activity fees are due in full by 5:00 PM on the Friday preceding the first day of instruction. Accounts with outstanding unpaid balances after this date will incur a late payment penalty of one hundred fifty dollars ($150) and a registration hold will be placed on the student account.

Clause 6.2 - Refund Schedule for Term Withdrawals:
Students who formally withdraw from the university receive tuition refunds based on the following schedule:
- Prior to the 1st day of classes: 100% refund (minus $50 processing fee)
- Days 1 through 7 of classes: 80% tuition refund
- Days 8 through 14 of classes: 50% tuition refund
- Days 15 through 28 of classes: 25% tuition refund
- After day 28 of classes: 0% tuition refund (No refunds provided)
Mandatory student fees, health insurance premiums, and housing deposits are strictly non-refundable after the first day of class.

Clause 6.3 - Delinquent Accounts and Transcripts:
Any student with an outstanding financial balance exceeding five hundred dollars ($500) will be barred from registering for subsequent terms, and requests for official academic transcripts or diplomas will be held until the balance is resolved in full.
